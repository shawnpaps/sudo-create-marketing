## The music industry’s network

Tourpass is a React web app for music industry professionals, with Convex powering its backend. It gives the people who make live music happen a dedicated place to build a professional presence and connect across roles.

The product is designed for artists, managers, booking agents, venues, producers, and the wider network around them. Instead of trying to turn a general-purpose social platform into an industry tool, Tourpass starts with the relationships and roles that already shape the music business.

## What Sudo Create is building

- Professional profiles shaped around music industry roles
- A focused network for finding and connecting with industry peers
- An experience that serves both onstage and behind-the-scenes professionals
- A React interface backed by Convex
- A product foundation that can grow with the Tourpass community
