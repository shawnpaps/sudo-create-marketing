## An industry built on relationships needs tools that fit.

Music professionals often keep track of people across social platforms, private messages, contact lists, and word of mouth. A recording engineer looking for an artist, a promoter looking for local bands, and a touring musician visiting a new city have different reasons to search. They all need enough context to know who’s worth reaching out to.

## We started with professional identity.

A profile on Tourpass is meant to tell someone what you do, where you work, and how you fit into the industry. Search and discovery draw on that context so people can find peers outside their immediate networks. A venue, an engineer, and a performer can each explain their work in terms the rest of the industry understands.

## Designing for a community.

A network has to make sense to people who come to it for different reasons. We used familiar patterns for profiles and search, then shaped the information around the roles and locations that matter in music. The goal was to give people enough context to start a useful conversation.

## Behind the build.

Tourpass is a full-stack web application built with React, Vite, and Convex. The application brings together authentication, structured professional profiles, and search. The separate public site explains the product and gives people a way into the beta.

## What we built.

- A professional networking web application for the music industry.
- Profiles organized around industry roles and professional information.
- Search and discovery for finding relevant people.
- Sign-in, account infrastructure, and structured application data.
- A public marketing site and beta signup.
