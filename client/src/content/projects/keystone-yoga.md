## A clearer introduction to the studio

Keystone Yoga needed its website to serve two kinds of visitors. Someone new to the studio might need help choosing a class. A returning student might just want to find the schedule or see what’s coming up.

## A complete redesign of the existing website

Sudo Create redesigned the site around those journeys. The homepage introduces the studio and its classes, with clear paths to class information and upcoming events. Visitors who are ready to practice can go straight to booking.

Shawn worked directly with Kristen throughout the redesign, shared progress as the work took shape, and walked her through the new features so she could use them after handoff.
