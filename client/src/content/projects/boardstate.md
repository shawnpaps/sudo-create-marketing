## Good software should leave room for a good game.

Players are handling physical cards, making decisions, talking to friends, and keeping track of shared information. The bookkeeping matters, but every fiddly interaction pulls a little attention away from the table. The challenge was making information easier to get to while keeping screen time brief.

## We treated it like tabletop equipment.

Players should be able to pick up the tool, understand what they need, and return their attention to the game. Legible information and straightforward controls matter because the app is used alongside cards, conversation, and everything else happening around the table.

That setting also informed the entry points. The public lobby offers a code-based way to join a game, and gives draft organizers and players distinct paths to get started.

## The principles behind Boardstate.

### Clarity over complexity

Make game information easy to read and keep the next action easy to find.

### Function over decoration

Use visual choices that help people understand the shared state and act quickly.

### People over screens

Support the game happening around the table. Give players a reason to put the device down when they’re done.

## What we built.

- A digital companion for Commander gameplay.
- Code-based game entry and controls for setting up the table.
- Dedicated entry points for running or joining a draft.
- A table-first interface designed for legibility and quick interactions.

Boardstate is an independent companion product.
