## More game, less bookkeeping

Boardstate is a companion app for Magic: The Gathering players. It is designed around the two places where a shared digital tool can be genuinely helpful: keeping a Commander table readable and supporting a smooth draft night.

The product treats the screen like another piece of tabletop equipment. Information stays visible, controls stay close at hand, and the interface supports the people around the table instead of asking for their attention.

## What Sudo Create is building

- A shared companion for Commander games
- Draft tools for groups playing together
- A table-first interface designed for quick, legible use
- A focused product that leaves room for the social part of the game
