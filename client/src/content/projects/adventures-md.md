## An inviting front door for a travel agency

Adventures MD needed a website that could turn the feeling of planning a trip into a clear, approachable online experience. The design pairs an editorial sense of place with practical paths for learning about the agency and starting a conversation.

Built in Framer, the site gives the business a polished, flexible home that can evolve as destinations, offers, and travel ideas change.

## What Sudo Create built

- A responsive marketing website in Framer
- A clear visual hierarchy for agency information and travel inspiration
- Reusable page sections that make future updates easier
- Direct calls to action for prospective travelers
