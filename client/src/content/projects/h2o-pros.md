## Finding a plumber should be straightforward.

A homeowner researching water filtration has different questions from someone planning a replacement or dealing with a burst pipe. The website had to serve all of them across a large service area, help new customers feel comfortable calling, and give the team a way to keep the site current as its marketing grew.

## Built around the homeowner.

We organized the site around the service a homeowner needs, the area they live in, and what they want to know about the people doing the work. Dedicated service pages explain individual offerings. Location pages connect those services to the communities the company serves. Reviews, FAQs, and easy-to-find booking links support the next decision.

The design puts the company’s people and neighborhood identity front and center, with clear navigation and short paths to requesting help.

## Designed for local discovery.

County and city pages give homeowners a way to find information specific to where they live. They also give the business a consistent place to add local details and connect them to relevant services. That structure makes the site easier to navigate and gives the team a foundation for local search and future content.

The current public sitemap lists nine service pages, nine county pages, and eighteen city pages.

## Behind the build.

Astro powers the public site. Payload CMS gives the team a structured way to manage services, locations, reviews, and other marketing content. Reusable layouts keep related pages consistent and let the team publish updates without rebuilding pages by hand. Neon provides the database infrastructure, and Vercel supports hosting and deployment.

## What we delivered.

- Responsive website design and development.
- Dedicated service pages and location landing pages.
- Online booking entry points and practical contact paths.
- Customer reviews, educational content, and FAQs.
- Structured content management and a foundation for local SEO.
